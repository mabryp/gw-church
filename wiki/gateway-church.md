# Gateway Church

**Summary:** Small, multi-cultural, discipleship-focused church in San Antonio, TX; the entity whose website (gw-church.org) this project is migrating off Google Sites.

## Identity

- **Address:** 6623 Five Palms Drive, San Antonio, TX 78242, United States
- **Phone:** (210) 674-5703
- **Email:** staff@gw-church.org
- **Domain:** gw-church.org (currently pointed at Google Sites)

## Sunday service

Facts supplied by the owner 2026-11-09 for the [Homepage](homepage.md):

- **Time:** Sundays at 10:00 a.m. (at the Five Palms Drive address above).
- **Worship:** mostly contemporary with traditional influences, then prayer and
  a sermon rooted in Scripture. The church describes itself as Bible-believing.
- **Families:** children's church and nursery care during the service.
- **Congregation:** describes itself as "a young, vibrant group." The earlier
  homepage's "multi-cultural, community-oriented" wording was dropped from the
  site at the owner's request (2026-11-09).

## Mission and voice

The site's messaging centers on discipleship in community: "Each of us is called
to be a disciple of Christ and to do this discipleship life in community."
Homepage themes: a place to be known, rest, heal, learn, grow, lead, make an
impact; multi-cultural, community-oriented, "room enough for everyone." Tagline
motif on the school page: a mission "to train and send." The homepage was
rewritten 2026-11-09 in a visitor-first voice aimed at believing families
seeking a biblically grounded church, tagline "Rooted in God's Word. A home
for your family." — see [Homepage](homepage.md); the discipleship sentence above no
longer appears on the live page but remains the church's self-description.

Lead pastor: [Chris Dumont](chris-dumont.md), who succeeded
[Charley](charley.md) (pastor 1981 until retirement, reported July 2026).
Doctrine:
[What We Believe](what-we-believe.md). Affiliated school:
[Gateway Christian School](gateway-christian-school.md). Outreach channels:
[Connect Channels](connect-channels.md).
