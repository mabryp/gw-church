# What We Believe (Statement of Faith)

**Summary:** [Gateway Church](gateway-church.md)'s statement of faith. Since 2026-11-09 it is a five-article confessional statement supplied by the owner, replacing the 11-point list from the Google Site; the site page at `/about/what-we-believe` reproduces the new text verbatim with every scripture reference linked.

## Current statement (five articles, adopted 2026-11-09)

Opening confession: "We are a local church centered on Jesus Christ, grounded
in Scripture, and committed to growing together as His disciples. Our faith
shapes how we worship, care for one another, and serve our neighbors."

| Article | Core claims | Scripture |
|---|---|---|
| God and His Word | One God, eternally Father, Son, Holy Spirit; creator and sustainer; people made in His image; Bible inspired, infallible, authoritative | Gen 1:26–27; Matt 28:19; 2 Tim 3:16–17 |
| Jesus Christ and Salvation | Fully God and fully man; virgin birth, sinless life, substitutionary death, bodily resurrection, ascension, intercession, return in power and glory; salvation by grace through faith in Christ alone; call to repent and believe is for everyone | Mark 1:14–15; 1 Cor 15:3–4; Eph 2:8–9; Heb 7:25 |
| The Holy Spirit | Fully God; convicts of sin, regenerates, indwells every believer; grows holiness; gives gifts for the church and the gospel | John 16:8; Titus 3:5; Eph 1:13–14; 1 Cor 12:4–7 |
| The Church and Our Calling | Community of believers: worship, learn and obey the Word, make disciples, care for one another; small, independent congregation led by a team of elders; love for God visible in love for neighbors, unity with believers, service to those in need | Matt 22:37–39; Matt 28:18–20; Acts 2:42–47; 1 Pet 4:10–11 |
| Our Hope | Jesus will return; resurrection of saved and lost; eternal life with Christ or eternal judgment; hope drives humble, urgent, loving evangelism | John 5:28–29; Rev 21:1–4 |

The verbatim text is in the source file and on the site page. The elder-led,
independent self-description in the fourth article is new information about
the church's governance not stated elsewhere on the site.

## Site page (`site/about/what-we-believe/index.html`)

- Layout: "creed" treatment. Opening confession set large in Playfair under a
  lime rule; the five articles follow in a two-column grid (title left, text
  right) separated by hairline rules; single column under 700px.
- Each scripture reference is a pill linking to Bible Gateway (default
  translation, opens in a new tab).
- Closing invitation with two buttons: "Plan a visit" (home) and "Connect
  with us" (`/connect`), matching the visitor-first [Homepage](homepage.md).
- Styles live in `site/css/style.css` under "What We Believe: creed layout";
  the old `.beliefs` numbered-list styles were removed.

## Previous statement (Google Site, through 2026-11-09)

An 11-point evangelical list, each point with scripture citations: the Bible
as inspired and infallible; one God in three persons; deity, virgin birth,
sinless life, miracles, substitutionary death, resurrection, ascension and
return of Christ; regeneration by the Spirit essential for salvation;
resurrection of saved and lost; Christ's imminent return; the Great
Commission; spiritual unity of believers; holy living with concern for social
needs; renewed dedication to the Lord's service; salvation by grace through
faith. Verbatim text with all citations remains in
`raw/about_what-we-believe.html`. The new statement covers the same ground in
prose; nothing in the old list is contradicted.
