# What We Believe (Statement of Faith)

**Summary:** [Gateway Church](gateway-church.md)'s 11-point evangelical statement of faith, each point with scripture citations; must be reproduced verbatim in the migrated site.

## The eleven points (abbreviated; verbatim text in source)

1. The Bible is the inspired, only infallible and authoritative Word of God.
2. One true God, eternally existent in three persons: Father, Son, Holy Spirit.
3. Deity of Jesus Christ: virgin birth, sinless life, miracles, substitutional
   death, bodily resurrection, ascension, personal return in power and glory.
4. Regeneration by the Holy Spirit essential for salvation; spiritual rebirth;
   godly living only through the Spirit's power.
5. Resurrection of both saved and lost — the saved to eternal life in heaven,
   the lost to hell.
6. Jesus Christ rose from the dead and is returning soon.
7. The Great Commission: preach the Gospel to every person.
8. Spiritual unity of believers through the Holy Spirit and Jesus Christ.
9. Holy Christian living, with concern for the hurts and social needs of others.
10. Renewed dedication to the Lord's service and His authority over our lives.
11. Salvation is a free gift by grace through faith.

Each point carries scripture references (e.g., point 1: 2 Tim. 3:15–4:1,
2 Thess. 2:13, 2 Peter 1:21–2:1). The full verbatim list with all citations is
in the source file — treat that as canonical when building the new site page.
