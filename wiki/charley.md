# Charley (Pastor)

**Summary:** Pastor of [Gateway Church](gateway-church.md) since 1981; Air Force Academy-trained, internationally invited discipleship teacher.

## Background

- Trained for leadership at the **Air Force Academy**, not seminary; served 8
  years in the Air Force before entering ministry.
- Began full-time ministry as a teacher at
  [Gateway Christian School](gateway-christian-school.md), then became Pastor of
  Gateway Church in **1981**.

## Life messages

"How To Hear God's Voice", "The School of Humility", "The One Necessary Thing",
"Sitting and Listening at His Feet", "The Mystery Hidden From The Ages — Christ
In You", "Something Worth Waiting For — The Spirit Upon".

## International ministry

- Invited by the **European Baptist Convention** to speak on discipleship at
  their annual convention in Switzerland.
- Multiple trips to **Peru**: trained 700 church leaders on discipleship on one
  trip; 3-day Spiritual Leadership seminar with 40 pastors on another.
- **August 2013, Uganda**: spoke 6 times at a conference of 2,000+ church
  leaders, plus 2 Sunday church services.
