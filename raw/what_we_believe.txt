# What We Believe

**We are a local church centered on Jesus Christ, grounded in Scripture, and committed to growing together as His disciples.** Our faith shapes how we worship, care for one another, and serve our neighbors.

### God and His Word

We believe there is one God, eternally existing as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. God created and sustains all things, made people in His image, and calls us into fellowship with Him. We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God. It guides what we teach and how we live. *(Genesis 1:26–27; Matthew 28:19; 2 Timothy 3:16–17)*

### Jesus Christ and Salvation

We believe Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man. Born of a virgin, He lived without sin, died in our place for our sins, rose bodily on the third day, and ascended to the Father, where He intercedes for His people. He will return in power and glory. Salvation is God’s gift of grace, received through faith in Jesus Christ alone. His call to repent and believe the gospel is for everyone. *(Mark 1:14–15; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4; Ephesians 2:8–9; Hebrews 7:25)*

### The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit is fully God. He convicts people of sin, brings new life to those who trust in Christ, and dwells in every believer. He helps us grow in holiness and gives gifts for the good of the church and the spread of the gospel. *(John 16:8; Titus 3:5; Ephesians 1:13–14; 1 Corinthians 12:4–7)*

### The Church and Our Calling

We believe the church is a community of believers called to worship God, learn and obey His Word, make disciples, and care for one another. As a small, independent congregation led by a team of elders, we share the work of ministry and seek to know and serve the people entrusted to our care. We want our love for God to be visible in our love for our neighbors, our unity with other believers, and our service to those in need. *(Matthew 22:37–39; Matthew 28:18–20; Acts 2:42–47; 1 Peter 4:10–11)*

### Our Hope

We believe Jesus will return and that God will raise both the saved and the lost. Those who belong to Christ will live forever with Him; those who reject Him will face eternal judgment. This hope moves us to share the gospel with humility, urgency, and love. *(John 5:28–29; Revelation 21:1–4)*
