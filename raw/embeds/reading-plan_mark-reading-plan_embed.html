Gospel Reading Plan · Year One

The Gospel of Mark

Follow Jesus through Mark’s fast-moving account of the Servant-King: acting with authority, showing compassion, suffering willingly, and triumphing over death.

7 Weeks July 13 – August 28, 2026 Monday–Friday Readings

How to Use This Plan

Read the assigned passage each weekday. Take time to notice what the text reveals about Jesus, how people respond to Him, and what faithful discipleship requires.

Old Testament Connections: Isaiah 40:3 · Psalm 2

Mark introduces Jesus with urgency and action. The promised Messiah immediately begins proclaiming the Kingdom, demonstrating divine authority through teaching, healing, forgiving sins, and calling ordinary people to follow Him.

Mark presents Jesus as the Servant who acts with authority, compassion, and sacrificial love. Luke now begins an orderly account, inviting us to understand with certainty who Jesus is and why His life changes everything.

The Three C’s of Reading

As you read each day, consider whether the passage leaves you convicted, confused, or confirmed.

Convicted What is God showing me that needs to change in my beliefs, attitudes, or actions?
Confused What do I not yet understand, and what question should I bring to further study?
Confirmed What truth about God, Christ, or faithful living has been strengthened in me?
