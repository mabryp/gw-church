Gospel Reading Plan · Year One

The Gospel of Luke

Follow Jesus through Luke’s compassionate account of the Savior who seeks the lost, welcomes the outcast, and brings good news to all people.

9 Weeks August 31 – October 30, 2026 Monday–Friday Readings

How to Use This Plan

Read the assigned passage each weekday. Take time to notice what the text reveals about Jesus, how people respond to Him, and what faithful discipleship requires.

Old Testament Connections: Isaiah 40 · 1 Samuel 2 · Micah 5

Luke opens his orderly account with songs, angels, and humble shepherds. The long-promised Savior arrives not in a palace but a manger, and from the very first chapter Luke shows God lifting up the lowly.

Rejected by the leaders, betrayed, and crucified between criminals, Jesus forgives even from the cross. Then the tomb is empty: hearts burn on the Emmaus road, and the risen Savior sends His witnesses to all nations.

The Three C’s of Reading

As you read each day, consider whether the passage leaves you convicted, confused, or confirmed.

Convicted What is God showing me that needs to change in my beliefs, attitudes, or actions?
Confused What do I not yet understand, and what question should I bring to further study?
Confirmed What truth about God, Christ, or faithful living has been strengthened in me?
