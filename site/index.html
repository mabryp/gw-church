Each of us is called to be a disciple of Christ and to do this discipleship life in community. At Gateway, you'll be welcomed in to a body of believers ready to minister to you and challenge you to discover the joy and satisfaction that comes from offering your life to the One who created you.

What You'll Find

Let us show you a place where you matter … to people and to God. Here — among our multi-cultural, community-oriented group of every day people — you will find a place to be known … to rest, heal, learn, grow, lead, and to make an impact.

We're Here for You

If you need prayer, or you're curious to learn more about becoming part of the Gateway family, we invite you to connect with us by email, phone, or social media.

At Gateway, there is room enough for everyone … and a place just for you.
