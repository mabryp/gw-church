Welcome to Gateway Church

Rooted in God’s Word. A home for your family.

Join us this Sunday

Sundays at 10:00 a.m. · 6623 Five Palms Drive, San Antonio

Plan Your Visit Watch a Sermon

Whether church has been part of your life for years or you’re considering a visit for the first time, we’d love to meet you. At Gateway, we gather to worship God, hear from the Bible, pray, and grow together as we follow Jesus.

You don’t need to know what to say or do before you come. Join us as you are, and take the time you need to get to know us.

What to expect

When you arrive, you’ll find people ready to welcome you and help you find your way. Our worship is mostly contemporary with traditional influences, followed by prayer and a sermon rooted in Scripture. We are a Bible-believing church. Families are welcome: we offer children’s church and nursery care during the service.

Life together

Church is more than a Sunday service. We’re a young, vibrant group who want to know one another, care for our neighbors, and help each other live out our faith throughout the week. There’s room to ask questions, build friendships, and find a way to take part — share a meal with us at Wednesday Dinner, or read through the Gospels alongside the congregation with our reading plan.

Here you will find a place to be known: to rest, heal, learn, grow, lead, and make an impact.
